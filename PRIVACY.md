# Privacy Policy for FocusTube

**Effective date:** September 4, 2026

FocusTube ("the extension", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the extension collects, stores, and shares.

## Summary

FocusTube does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It works entirely in your browser, on your device.

## Data the extension stores

FocusTube stores only the settings you choose, locally in your browser:

| Data | Where | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Focus mode on/off state (per tab) | Chrome/Edge `storage.session` | Restores focus mode if you switch between videos |
| Dim level (0–100) | Chrome/Edge `storage.sync` | Remembers your preferred overlay brightness |

- **`storage.session`** data is stored only in your current browser session and is cleared when you close the browser.
- **`storage.sync`** data is stored in your browser profile and may sync to other devices signed into the same browser account. It contains only your dim-level preference (a number from 0 to 100). No videos, browsing history, or personal information is stored.

## Data the extension does NOT collect

FocusTube does **not** collect, log, or transmit:
- Browsing history
- Video URLs or watch history
- Personal information (name, email, location)
- IP addresses or device identifiers
- Cookies or tracking identifiers

## Network access

The extension makes **no** network requests of its own. It does not contact any server, analytics service, or third party. The only network interaction is the page it runs on (YouTube.com), and the extension only modifies the display of that page.

## Permissions

FocusTube requests the following permissions and how they are used:

| Permission | Use |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your dim-level preference and per-tab focus state, as described above |
| `activeTab` | Lets the toolbar/popup interact with the currently active tab when you click the FocusTube icon |

## Third-party services

None. FocusTube does not use advertising, analytics, or any third-party code.

## Children's privacy

FocusTube does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this document will be revised. Material changes will be reflected in the extension's store listing description.
